# Observe the analyst before demonstrating MetricGuard

**Case ID:** ___  **Date:** ___  **Observer:** ___  **Analyst role (no private identity required):** ___  **Observation type:** live / retrospective (mark estimates)

Explain that the goal is to understand the analyst's existing process, not to grade them. Ask them to work as they normally would. Do not show MetricGuard, its six steps, or a proposed explanation until the baseline observation is complete. Record what is actually seen separately from later recollection.

## Opening prompts

- What business question triggered this investigation? Who needed the answer, and why did the discrepancy matter?
- What were the two original reports or sources, exact displayed values, units, reporting periods, filters, and refresh/cutoff times? Capture a permissible reference or redacted copy.
- What did the analyst expect before investigating? What business conditions must hold for the two numbers to be comparable?
- Where did they find each definition and the relevant data? Note access, permissions, extraction steps, and uncertainty about the source version.

## Observe the work as it happens

Log events in order without forcing the app's stages onto them. For each event note start/end time, active effort, waiting, tool/source, action, hypothesis, finding, and what prompted the next move. Capture loops between querying, inspecting records, asking a colleague, revising filters, checking report settings, and changing hypotheses. Note any work delegated or delayed by access. Record when the analyst asks for help and what help changes.

## Resolution and handoff

- What explanation or resolution existed before this observation, if any? For a resolved case, keep the known explanation from the investigator where practical and record who held it.
- Which original endpoint values were independently checked? Was one or both values still unexplained?
- What evidence did the recipient or reviewer require to accept the conclusion: query, report settings, row examples, amount bridge, source revision, owner decision, or another artifact?
- How was the explanation communicated? Did the recipient understand it and request follow-up?
- Which parts would the analyst repeat next time? What remains uncertain?

After this baseline observation, introduce MetricGuard and use the [case intake](case-intake.md) to decide whether the same question fits its supported workflow. Keep the baseline notes intact; do not rewrite them to match the product's steps.
